# Code of Conduct

AriaAI is an open-source project for people exploring AI-native professional workspaces. We want discussions to be useful, respectful, and grounded in the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be clear when something is unsafe, incorrect, or harmful.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Welcome people who are new to the project.
- Protect private data, customer context, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, customer documents, or confidential project data.
- Repeatedly derailing technical or product discussions.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and community channels connected to AriaAI.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close discussions; block users; or take other reasonable action to protect the project and its contributors.

If you see behavior that makes the project unsafe or unproductive, contact the maintainer privately.
