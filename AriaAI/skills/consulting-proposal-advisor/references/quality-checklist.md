# Quality Checklist

Run this checklist before delivering a proposal, memo, PPT outline, or PPTX.

## Scoring

Score major deliverables out of 20 before final delivery. A client-ready draft should score at least 16, with no zero in any category.

- Story and recommendation: 0-4
- Specificity and client relevance: 0-4
- Evidence and assumption discipline: 0-4
- Execution practicality: 0-4
- Executive readability and polish: 0-4

Use this interpretation:

- 18-20: client-ready with minor polishing
- 16-17: usable draft, disclose assumptions or weak spots
- 13-15: internal draft only; revise before client use
- 0-12: not ready; rebuild story or evidence base

## Story

- The first page states the recommendation clearly.
- The proposal answers why now, why this solution, and why this team or approach.
- The document has a logical chain: context -> diagnosis -> recommendation -> execution -> value -> next steps.
- Every major section supports the client's decision.

## Specificity

- Client facts are separated from assumptions.
- Generic claims are replaced with concrete outcomes.
- Workstreams have deliverables and owners.
- Timeline includes milestones and decision gates.
- Risks include practical mitigations.

## Executive Readability

- Headings carry the message.
- Bullets are short and parallel.
- The proposal can be skimmed in 3 minutes.
- The executive summary stands alone.
- Next steps are explicit and time-bound.

## Consulting Rigor

- Alternatives and tradeoffs are acknowledged.
- Value model assumptions are visible.
- Scope boundaries are clear.
- Dependencies on client data, access, and stakeholders are stated.
- No unsupported claims, invented credentials, or fake benchmarks appear.

## PPT Delivery

- The deck uses a clean self-owned consulting template unless the user explicitly supplied a template.
- The deck is built from the story, not from a copied reference deck.
- Every slide has a conclusion-led title and one primary purpose.
- Every analytical slide has a proof object and client implication.
- Standard-depth decks are at least 16 slides unless the user explicitly requested a quick draft.
- The deck contains no old client names, old industry terms, source-template labels, hidden placeholders, or irrelevant reference content.
- The PPTX opens or exports to PDF successfully.
- Charts, tables, and shapes are editable where possible.

## Revision Triggers

Revise before delivery if any of these are true:

- The recommendation could apply to almost any client.
- The executive summary does not name a decision or action.
- Any slide title is only a topic label, such as `背景`, `市场分析`, `解决方案`, or `项目计划`.
- More than two analytical slides are bullet-only.
- A chapter has recommendations but no supporting diagnosis or proof object.
- Value is stated without assumptions or measurement logic.
- Risks are generic and have no owner or mitigation.
- PPT contains copied reference content or unexplained placeholders.

## Chinese Proposal Polish

- Use business Chinese, not literal English translation.
- Prefer concise phrases: `核心判断`, `方案建议`, `实施路径`, `价值测算`, `风险保障`, `下一步`.
- Avoid vague slogans such as `赋能升级`, `全面提升`, or `打造闭环` unless explained concretely.
