## Huawei 5-See 3-Define

Best for: Large enterprises (>5B RMB revenue), business-led transformations, complex multi-business-group organizations.

### 5-See (五看) — Strategic Analysis

| See | Focus | Key Questions |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **See Industry/Trend** (看行业/趋势) | Macro environment, technology trends, policy direction | What technologies will disrupt this industry in 5-10 years? What regulatory changes are coming? |
| **See Market** (看市场) | Customer needs, market size, growth segments | What digital services do customers now expect? Which segments are growing fastest? |
| **See Customers** (看客户) | Customer journey, pain points, digital behavior | Where do customers experience friction? What digital channels do they prefer? |
| **See Competitors** (看竞争) | Digital maturity of competitors, best practices | Who is the digital leader in this industry? What capabilities differentiate them? |
| **See Self** (看自己) | Internal capabilities, assets, constraints | What are our core digital strengths? Where are the critical gaps? |

### 3-Define (三定) — Strategic Decisions

| Define | Output | Key Decisions |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Define Strategy** (定战略) | Digital vision, strategic positioning | What type of digital enterprise will we become? (efficiency-driven / customer-centric / ecosystem platform) |
| **Define Goals** (定目标) | Capability targets, KPIs | Which capabilities to build? What maturity level by when? |
| **Define Path** (定路径) | Implementation roadmap | Phased approach, priority initiatives, resource allocation |

### Huawei Framework Strengths
- Strong at linking business strategy to digital initiatives
- Emphasizes external perspective (market, customer, competition) before internal assessment
- Well-tested in Chinese enterprise context (SMEs to large SOEs)
- Provides clear decision gates at each step

### Common Pitfalls
- 5-See analysis can become too broad; constrain scope with clear industry boundaries
- Avoid defining strategy purely top-down; include pilot feedback loops

## McKinsey Digital Quotient

Best for: Mid-size enterprises, rapid digital build-outs, organizations needing quick diagnostic.

### Digital Quotient Assessment

4 dimensions, 18 management practices:

| Dimension | Practices | Weight |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Strategy** | Digital vision clarity, strategy communication, resource allocation | 25% |
| **Culture** | Risk tolerance, collaboration, customer-centricity, agility | 25% |
| **Organization** | Talent acquisition, digital skills, organizational structure, governance | 25% |
| **Capabilities** | Digital channels, data & analytics, technology architecture, innovation | 25% |

### 3-Horizon Model for Digital Initiatives

| Horizon | Timeframe | Focus | Governance |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **H1** | 0-12 months | Optimize core business with digital | Business unit led |
| **H2** | 1-3 years | Build new digital capabilities | Central digital team + business unit |
| **H3** | 3-5 years | Create breakthrough digital business models | Innovation lab / separate entity |

### McKinsey Framework Strengths
- Fast assessment (can complete in 2-3 weeks)
- Strong benchmark database (cross-industry comparisons available)
- Clear linkage between digital maturity and financial performance
- 3-Horizon model provides intuitive initiative categorization

## KPMG-Alibaba CPG Framework

Best for: Consumer-facing industries (retail, FMCG, consumer electronics), platform/ecosystem strategies.

### CPG Digital Maturity Model

4 dimensions, 18 sub-dimensions:

| Dimension | Sub-dimensions |
|:---|:---|
| **Consumer Operations** (消费者运营) | Consumer insight, precision marketing, membership management, omni-channel experience |
| **Product Innovation** (产品创新) | Consumer-driven R&D, digital product development, agile product management |
| **Supply Chain** (供应链) | Demand forecasting, intelligent procurement, smart manufacturing, digital logistics |
| **Organization & Data** (组织与数据) | Data governance, talent, agile organization, data platform |

### Maturity Levels

- L1 Channel Digital — Online presence, basic e-commerce
- L2 Marketing Digital — Data-driven marketing, consumer insights
- L3 Supply Chain Digital — End-to-end supply chain integration
- L4 Full Enterprise Digital — Organization-wide digital transformation
- L5 Ecosystem Digital — Platform-based ecosystem operation

## EBC Model

Best for: Enterprises evaluating ERP-to-EBC evolution, organizations building composable business capabilities.

### 5-Step EBC Growth Model

1. **Connect** — Link business processes across functional silos
2. **Process** — Automate and optimize end-to-end processes
3. **Insight** — Generate real-time insights from operational data
4. **Innovate** — Enable rapid innovation through composable capabilities
5. **Ecosystem** — Extend capabilities to external partners and customers

### EBC vs Traditional ERP

| Aspect | ERP | EBC |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Focus | Internal efficiency | Customer value + ecosystem |
| Architecture | Monolithic | Composable, API-first |
| Data | Reporting | Real-time insights + action |
| Innovation | Slow, IT-led | Fast, business-led |

## Industry 4.0 Maturity Model

Best for: Manufacturing, industrial enterprises, smart factory initiatives.

### 6 Maturity Levels

| Level | Name | Characteristics |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 0 | Computerization | Standalone IT systems |
| 1 | Connectivity | Systems connected, data collected |
| 2 | Visibility | Real-time dashboards, data visualization |
| 3 | Transparency | Root cause analysis, predictive models |
| 4 | Predictive Capacity | Predictive analytics, autonomous decisions |
| 5 | Adaptability | Self-optimizing, autonomous systems |

### 4 Core Principles

1. **Interoperability** — Machines, devices, sensors, people connected
2. **Information Transparency** — Digital twin of physical processes
3. **Technical Assistance** — Systems support humans in decision-making
4. **Decentralized Decisions** — Autonomous systems make decisions

## Assessment Questionnaire Templates

### Generic Digital Maturity Questionnaire (18 questions, 6 dimensions)

**Strategy (3 questions)**
1. Is there a board-approved digital transformation strategy?
2. Is the digital strategy reviewed and updated annually?
3. Are digital initiatives linked to business outcomes with clear KPIs?

**Customer (3 questions)**
4. Are customer journeys mapped across all touchpoints?
5. Is customer data unified in a single platform/view?
6. Are digital channels generating >20% of revenue?

**Operations (3 questions)**
7. Are core business processes automated (>50%)?
8. Is real-time operational data available to frontline workers?
9. Are AI/ML models deployed in production for operations?

**Organization (3 questions)**
10. Is there a CDO or equivalent role with clear authority?
11. What % of employees have basic digital literacy? (>80% = advanced)
12. Are agile teams operating in business units?

**Data (3 questions)**
13. Is there a data governance framework with DPO?
14. Is data quality monitored and reported?
15. Are advanced analytics (predictive/prescriptive) in use?

**Technology (3 questions)**
16. Is core infrastructure cloud-based (>50%)?
17. Are systems API-enabled for integration?
18. Is there a DevOps/SRE practice for critical applications?

### Scoring

- 0 = Not started
- 1 = Initial/pilot
- 2 = Partial implementation
- 3 = Enterprise-wide implementation
- 4 = Optimized/continuous improvement

Score per dimension: sum of 3 questions / 12 × 100 = maturity %
