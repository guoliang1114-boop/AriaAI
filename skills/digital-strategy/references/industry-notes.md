## Manufacturing

### Typical Digital Capabilities

| Capability | Key Scenarios | Technologies |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Smart Factory | Predictive maintenance, quality vision inspection, digital work instructions | IoT, AI/ML, Digital Twin, MES |
| Digital Supply Chain | Demand sensing, autonomous planning, supplier collaboration | Control Tower, AI Planning, Blockchain |
| Connected Products | Remote monitoring, predictive service, usage-based pricing | IoT Platform, Edge Computing |
| Digital R&D | Simulation-driven design, digital validation, PLM integration | CAE/CFD, Digital Twin, VR/AR |

### Measurement inputs

Before setting targets, collect the definition, baseline, measurement period, segment, and owner for:

- OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)
- Unplanned Downtime
- First-Pass Quality Yield
- Supply Chain Planning Cycle
- Digital Thread Coverage

Compare only like-for-like data. Without a verified source, do not label values as industry average, leader, or a named company result.

### Common Pitfalls
- **OT-IT convergence failure**: Separate teams, budgets, and timelines for operational technology and IT; no unified architecture
- **Pilot purgatory**: Multiple successful pilots that never scale due to lack of change management and funding model
- **MES before process standardization**: Automating broken processes amplifies inefficiencies

Use a named company example only after verifying an original source, publication date, scope, and metric definition. If evidence is unavailable, discuss the capability pattern without company attribution or numerical performance claims.

| Smart Manufacturing | Continuous manufacturing, PAT (Process Analytical Technology), serialization | MES, IoT, Digital Twin |
| Patient Engagement | Adherence monitoring, remote patient monitoring, digital therapeutics | Connected Devices, Digital Biomarkers, App |
| Real-World Evidence | RWE data platform, outcomes research, regulatory-grade RWD | NLP, FHIR, Data Lake |

### Measurement inputs

Before setting targets, collect the definition, baseline, measurement period, segment, and owner for:

- Clinical Trial Digitization
- Patient Recruitment Speed
- Manufacturing Deviation Rate

- Time to Regulatory Submission

Compare only like-for-like data. Without a verified source, do not label values as industry average, leader, or a named company result.

### Common Pitfalls
- **Privacy and data access**: Confirm permitted use, minimum necessary data, and applicable obligations with the project owner before sharing data or training models
- **Clinical vs commercial silos**: Digital transformation in clinical (R&D) and commercial operate independently
- **Legacy validation burden**: Computerized systems validation (CSV) processes designed for on-premise software don't adapt to cloud/SaaS
